# Conceivable Life Sciences

> Conceivable Life Sciences builds AURA, an AI-powered, end-to-end automated in vitro
> fertilization (IVF) laboratory: six integrated robotic workstations that use machine vision,
> advanced optics and AI inference to automate more than 200 manual embryology steps. The company
> was founded in 2021, raised a $50M Series A in September 2025, opened the first automated IVF lab
> in Mexico City, and has partnered with IVI RMA Global to bring AURA to a US clinic. There is no
> public API, developer portal, SDK, webhook surface or machine-readable specification — the
> product is a physical laboratory platform sold to fertility clinics, not a software service.

## AURA workstations

- [C:SPERM](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-sperm): Automated sperm preparation and selection.
- [C:EGG](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-egg): Oocyte finding and denudation.
- [C:ICSI](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-icsi): Fertilization and micromanipulation.
- [C:DISH](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-dish): Automated dish preparation.
- [C:CULTURE](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-culture): Incubation and embryo culture.
- [C:VIT](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-vit): Cryopreservation and vitrification.
- [C:HANDLER](https://www.conceivable.life/ivf-tech/c-handler): Sample handling and transfer.
