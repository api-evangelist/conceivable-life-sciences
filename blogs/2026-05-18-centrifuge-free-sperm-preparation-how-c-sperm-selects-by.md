Nature selects sperm by swimming — only motile sperm reach the egg, everything else falls away. Yet in most IVF labs, that process is replaced by centrifugation, which damages healthy sperm before the embryologist ever touches the sample. The harm happens at the molecular level, invisible under any microscope.
