Visual sperm selection for ICSI has long relied on an embryologist's trained eye — but human assessment can't precisely measure motility parameters like velocity and path linearity. Conceivable's SiD technology adds objective, real-time motility data to the selection process, with four independent studies showing improved outcomes, especially for older patients and less experienced embryologists.
